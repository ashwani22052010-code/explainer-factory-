A farmer gets bitten by a snake in the rice field at night. His family rushes him to the village healer. Leaves, chants, a rope tied tight around his leg. And he survived. So does it work? Or is it something else? Most snakes are not venomous. And even venomous snakes don't inject venom every time they bite. A bite with no venom is called a dry bite. So the healer gets the credit, but no venom saved him. Now imagine the bite was venomous. Precious hours are lost while the venom spreads through the body. The only real cure is anti-venom, at a hospital. Not a rope. Not a chant. If a snake bites someone, go straight to the hospital.
